02 · Micro-learning 1

What is a procedure text?

A procedure text tells us how to do or make something through clear, ordered instructions.

📘 4 min
🍳

Its purpose

To explain how to make, do, or use something so readers or listeners can follow the steps.

🧭

Where can we find it?

Recipes, craft instructions, science experiments, user guides, and directions for activities.

A procedure text can be about…

1
Making somethingHow to make a fruit salad.
2
Doing somethingHow to wash your hands correctly.
3
Explaining a processHow a simple process happens, step by step.
This learning adventure focuses on a recipe. Procedure texts can also explain processes connected to personal experiences or historical events, but the same key idea remains: explain the sequence clearly.

04 · Micro-learning 3

Words that make instructions work

Use action words, sequence words, and clear commands.

🔤 5 min

💪 Action verbs

These words tell the reader what to do.

cutmixpouraddstirboilfryserve

➡️ Sequence words

These words show the order of actions.

FirstNextThenAfter thatFinally

🔎 Notice the language!

First, peel the bananas. Next, mix the flour and water. Then, dip the bananas into the batter. Finally, fry them until golden.

📌 Language features

  • Imperative sentences: “Cut the onion.” / “Add some salt.”
  • Ingredients and quantities: “two eggs”, “a cup of milk”.
  • Simple present form: “The mixture becomes thick.”
  • Clear sequence: use ordering words to avoid confusion.
Safety reminder: ask an adult for help when using a knife, stove, hot oil, or other kitchen equipment.
05 · Micro-learning 4

Read the secret recipe

Let's see how the three parts and language features work together.

🍌 5 min
SAMPLE RECIPE

How to Make Banana Fritters

Ingredients:
  • 2 ripe bananas
  • 5 tablespoons of flour
  • 100 ml of water
  • A little sugar and salt
  • Cooking oil
Steps:
  1. First, peel the bananas and cut them into small pieces.
  2. Next, mix the flour, water, sugar, and salt in a bowl.
  3. Then, dip the banana pieces into the batter.
  4. After that, ask an adult to fry them in hot oil until golden brown.
  5. Finally, drain the fritters and serve them on a plate.

06 · Formative assessment

Chef's quick challenge!

Answer all four questions. Choose one answer for each question and read the feedback.

🏅 4 questions

1. What is the main purpose of a procedure text?

A procedure text gives ordered instructions so readers can do or make something.

2. Which part tells us the things we need?

Ingredients or materials list the items needed, often with quantities.

3. Which word is an action verb?

“Stir” is an action verb. It tells someone what action to do.

4. Which sequence word usually introduces the last step?

“Finally” signals the final action or step.

✅ Success criteria

  • I include a clear title / goal.
